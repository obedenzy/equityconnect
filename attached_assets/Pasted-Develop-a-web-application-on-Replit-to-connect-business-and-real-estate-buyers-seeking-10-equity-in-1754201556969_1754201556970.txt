Develop a web application on Replit to connect business and real estate buyers seeking 10% equity injections for SBA and non-SBA acquisition loans with investors willing to provide the funds. The platform will facilitate connections through a listing-based system, charging listing fees to buyers, with features like tiered investment groups, video call integration, secure LOI and termsheet uploads, and inbox-style communication. The app will support both business and real estate equity injection markets, with no SEC compliance or legal liability, acting solely as a connection platform.

Functional Requirements





User Roles:





Users can register as either Buyers or Investors (not both).



Buyers can post funding requests for business or real estate equity injections.



Investors can browse and respond to requests but cannot post funding requests.



Tiered Investment Groups:





Funding requests are categorized into tiers based on the equity injection amount:





$100,000 to $350,000



$350,001 to $1,000,000



$1,000,001 to $3,000,000



$3,000,001 to $25,000,000



Separate listings for Business Equity Injection and Real Estate Equity Injection.



LOI and Termsheet Uploads:





Buyers must upload a Letter of Intent (LOI) and termsheet when posting a funding request.



Buyers can redact sensitive information (e.g., company name, seller name, address) in the uploaded documents until they grant access to specific investors.



Investors must receive a code via email and enter it, along with typing their name and clicking "Accept," to view the LOI and termsheet.



Investors can view documents but cannot download them until the buyer grants download access.



Communication:





All communication occurs via an inbox-style system within the platform (not real-time chat).



No email addresses are exposed to users.



Buyers and investors communicate through messages, with the option to initiate video calls for discussions.



Video Call Integration:





Integrate a third-party video call API (e.g., Whereby or Vonage) to allow buyers and investors to schedule and conduct video calls within the platform, reducing email exchanges.



Video calls are initiated from the inbox system, with links generated for scheduled meetings.



Investor Preferences:





Investors can specify their preferred return type: repayment, equity, or both.



Investors can filter funding requests by tier, market (business or real estate), and return type.



Listing Fee Structure:





Buyers pay a listing fee to post a funding request.



Suggested fee structure (adjustable based on market research):





$100,000–$350,000 tier: $500 per listing



$350,001–$1,000,000 tier: $1,000 per listing



$1,000,001–$3,000,000 tier: $2,500 per listing



$3,000,001–$25,000,000 tier: $5,000 per listing



No transaction fees or commissions to avoid legal liability or SEC compliance.



Fees are collected via a payment gateway (e.g., Stripe) upon posting.



Platform Liability:





The platform acts solely as a connection facilitator, not a broker or financial intermediary.



Buyers are responsible for redacting sensitive information in LOI and termsheet documents.



Clear terms of service stating the platform is not liable for legal issues or transaction outcomes.
